# AI Safe-Use Policy
## Hermes Control Graph — AI-Assisted GRC Workflow

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Human-Approved  
**Author:** Andrew Rogers  
**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026  

---

## Purpose

This policy governs the use of AI tools in the Hermes Control Graph
workflow. It defines what AI may and may not do, where human review
is required, how AI outputs are labeled, and what privacy boundaries
apply at every stage.

---

## AI tools in scope

| Tool | Role | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Perplexity Pro | Locate official source authorities and regulatory materials | Stage 1 |
| Claude | Draft proposed control mappings and rationale | Stage 2 |
| Gemini | Challenge mappings, identify missing conditions, flag unsupported conclusions | Stage 3 |
| Deterministic validator | Enforce required fields before acceptance | Stage 4 |
| Human reviewer | Approve, reject, or revise | Stage 5 |
| GitHub | Version control with reviewer decision and change log | Stage 6 |
| Hermes Relay | Hash-chained attestation of approved records | Stage 7 |

---

## What AI may do

- Propose draft control mappings with rationale
- Suggest framework identifiers and source authority links
- Draft evidence dictionary fields and descriptions
- Generate candidate evaluation test cases
- Summarize regulatory requirements in plain language
- Identify potential gaps or missing conditions in a proposed mapping
- Propose confidence scores with supporting rationale

---

## What AI may not do

- Release or publish any mapping without human review and approval
- Assert that a control fully satisfies a framework requirement
- Assert that any system is certified or compliant under any framework
- Produce, handle, or suggest real PHI or personal data in any form
- Include PHI, canary values, or sensitive configuration in any output
- Bypass or shortcut the human review gate for any reason
- Override a deterministic validator rejection
- Mark any record as human-approved without a documented human decision
- Reproduce proprietary framework control text

---

## Required fields before any mapping is accepted

Every AI-proposed mapping must include all of the following before
it may proceed to human review:

- [ ] Source framework name and version
- [ ] Framework control identifier
- [ ] Source authority URL
- [ ] Retrieval or effective date
- [ ] Mapping direction
- [ ] Confidence score
- [ ] Human-written rationale
- [ ] Disclaimer that the mapping is illustrative and requires assessor review

Any mapping missing one or more required fields is rejected by the
deterministic validator and returned to the drafting stage.

---

## Mapping status labels

Every mapping record must carry one of the following status labels
at all times:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ai-proposed` | Generated by AI — not yet validated or reviewed |
| `validated` | Passed deterministic field validation — awaiting human review |
| `human-approved` | Reviewed and approved by a named human reviewer |
| `released` | Approved and committed to version control with change log |
| `superseded` | Replaced by a newer version — retained for audit history |

No mapping may move from `validated` to `human-approved` without a
documented human reviewer name, decision, and date.

---

## Human review requirements

Human review is required before any of the following:

- A mapping moves from `validated` to `human-approved`
- A mapping is committed to version control as `released`
- An exception record is closed
- A detector fail finding is resolved
- An evaluation case result is marked as final
- Any AI output is presented to an external party

Human review must be documented with:
- Reviewer name
- Review date
- Decision: approve / reject / revise
- Notes on any changes made

---

## Privacy boundaries

These boundaries apply to all AI tools at all stages and may not
be overridden for any reason:

1. No real PHI or personal data may appear in any AI input or output
2. No real patient names, dates of birth, SSNs, MRNs, or other
   identifiers may be used in testing, examples, or demonstrations
3. All test inputs must use synthetic, non-realistic canary patterns
4. Canary values must never appear in evidence receipts, Slack alerts,
   or external notifications
5. Slack alerts must contain only the finding ID
6. Evidence receipts must record interception confirmation — not the
   intercepted content
7. No AI tool may be prompted with real customer or patient data
8. AI outputs containing accidental PHI must be immediately discarded
   and the input reviewed

## Confidence score definitions

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `high` | Strong direct alignment — the control mechanism directly satisfies the framework requirement |
| `medium-high` | Good alignment with minor gaps or indirect satisfaction |
| `medium` | Partial alignment — the control contributes but does not fully satisfy the requirement alone |
| `low` | Weak or indirect alignment — requires significant additional controls |
| `unsupported` | No defensible mapping exists without additional scope analysis |

AI must not assign `high` confidence to an indirect mapping.
AI must not assign any confidence score to a mapping that requires
qualified assessor analysis before a claim can be made.

---

## Evaluation and regression

- AI mapping outputs are evaluated against the gold standard dataset
  in `evaluations/gold-set.jsonl`
- Results are recorded in `evaluations/results.csv` after every
  significant model or prompt change
- Precision, recall, false positive rate, and false negative rate
  are tracked across versions
- Any regression in accuracy of more than 5% triggers a mandatory
  human review of all affected mappings before the next release
- The human override rate is tracked and reviewed quarterly

---

## Release threshold

No AI-assisted mapping content may be released until:

- All required fields are present and validated
- Human review is documented
- Accuracy on the gold set is at or above 0.80 average
- False negative rate on privacy boundary cases is 0.00
- No unsupported certification or compliance claims are present
- The disclaimer is present on all mapping outputs

---

## Prohibited outputs

AI must refuse and must never produce:

- Real PHI in any form
- Certification claims for any framework
- Full compliance assertions from a single control
- Legally authoritative interpretations of regulatory requirements
- Proprietary framework control text
- Canary values in any external output
- Mapping outputs without source authority URLs
